  • Kein Tracking, keine Analyse-Werkzeuge, keine Konten.

Privacy

  • Your photos of clothes stay on your phone.
  • Cutting out the clothes also happens on your phone.
  • A resized photo is sent for recognition only after you agree and tap “Send”.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no accounts.
